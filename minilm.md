1. Upgrade to the 12-Layer VariantMany developers default to all-MiniLM-L6-v2 because it is incredibly fast. However, if your primary goal is accuracy, swap to microsoft/MiniLM-L12-H384-uncased.It doubles the hidden layers from 6 to 12.It maintains the same fast execution speeds but retains much more complex contextual reasoning, which is vital for catching subtle disinformation.
2. Implement Hybrid Input (Title + Text + Source)MiniLM's maximum sequence length is 512 tokens. If you truncate long articles, you throw away critical context. If you only look at the body, you miss clickbait headlines. Combine your text fields intelligently before feeding them to the tokenizer:Format your text like this: f"Title: {title} | Source: {domain} | Body: {text[:400]}"Including the domain name or source (if available in your dataset) dramatically boosts accuracy because models naturally learn which domains frequently publish junk
3. Use Discriminative Fine-Tuning (Layer-Wise LR)Instead of updating every layer of MiniLM with the exact same learning rate, use Layer-Wise Learning Rate Decay (LLRD).Keep the lower layers (which understand basic English grammar) at a very low learning rate (e.g., 1e-5).Give the top classification head a higher learning rate (e.g., 5e-5).This prevents "catastrophic forgetting," allowing the model to adapt perfectly to fake news patterns without breaking its foundational language skills.
4. Advanced Data Cleaning (Crucial for Fake News)Fake news datasets are notorious for containing hidden artifacts that cause the model to cheat rather than learn. Clean your text strictly before training:Remove explicit publisher tags: Many "Real" articles in datasets like ISOT start with "(Reuters) - " or "(AP) - ". MiniLM will simply look for the word "Reuters" to mark an article as true. Strip these text prefixes out entirely.Normalize URLs and text: Replace all specific URLs with a generic [URL] token and usernames with [USER].
5. Train with Lookahead + AdamW OptimizerStandard AdamW can get stuck in local minima when fine-tuning distilled models. Use the Lookahead optimizer wrapped around AdamW, and apply a linear learning rate scheduler with warmup for the first 10% of your training steps. This stabilizes the weights and guarantees a more accurate convergence.
